# Reasoning

## Responsibility

Evaluates assembled evidence through structured reasoning — evidence evaluation, perspective evaluation, assumption evaluation, and blind spot evaluation.

## Architecture Reference

- **Primary:** `architecture/7 - ApexOS - Inference Architecture v1.0.docx` (Evidence Evaluation, Perspective Evaluation, Assumption Evaluation, Blind Spot Evaluation)

## Key Principles

- **Perspective Neutrality** — no perspective receives automatic authority (CP-008)
- **Assumption Transparency** — assumptions must remain visible and challengeable (AF-012)
- **Reflection Principle** — executive, stakeholders, and ApexOS are all subject to scrutiny

## Evaluative Standard

The objective is not to determine who is right. The objective is to determine what is most supported by evidence and most likely to improve outcomes/results.
