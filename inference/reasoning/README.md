## Key Principles

- **Perspective Neutrality (CP-008)** — no perspective receives automatic authority
- **Assumption Transparency (AF-012)** — assumptions must remain visible and challengeable
- **Reflection Principle** — executive, stakeholders, and ApexOS are all subject to scrutiny

## Evaluative Standard

The objective is not to determine who is right. The objective is to determine what is most supported by evidence and most likely to improve outcomes/results.

## Category Rule

Component artifacts in this folder contain evaluation — not findings (those consolidate in Interpretation Package) and not recommendations.
