# Amendment Controls

## Responsibility

Govern the evolution of doctrine and architecture — ensuring continuous improvement without architectural drift.

## Architecture Reference

- **Primary:** `architecture/6 - ApexOS - Governance Architecture v1.0.docx` (Continuous Improvement Principle)
- **Charter:** `architecture/1 - ApexOS - Project Charter v1.0.docx` (Section 15 — Evolution, Amendments & Governance)
- **Index:** `architecture/99 - ApexOS - Minimum Viable Index v2.0.docx` (Section 10 — Amendment Rule, FAB-001)

## Amendment Rule (Index Section 10)

Any proposed change to doctrine or architecture must:

1. Identify the affected source document
2. Identify the affected doctrine, principle, or decision
3. Provide rationale for amendment
4. Describe expected impact
5. Preserve visibility of all material changes

## Open Clarifications

**FAB-001 — Amendment Management Architecture** is listed as open backlog. The Index amendment rule and Charter Section 15 provide governing requirements. Full amendment workflow architecture is not yet complete — do not invent amendment processes beyond these sources.

## Principle

Improvement should remain possible. Transformation should remain visible.
